THREAT TRACKERS SCAM KNOWLEDGE BASE

OTP SCAMS
Scammers may pretend to be bank employees, customer-support agents, delivery agents, or government officials and ask for an OTP or verification code.
Never share an OTP, PIN, CVV, password, or verification code with another person.
A legitimate organization should not require you to reveal your OTP.

BANK AND KYC SCAMS
Scammers may claim that a bank account, card, or KYC registration will be blocked or suspended unless the victim acts immediately.
Urgency combined with requests for OTPs, passwords, card information, or payments is a major warning sign.
Verify the request through the bank's official website, application, or customer-support number.

PAYMENT SCAMS
Scammers may pressure victims to transfer money, pay processing fees, or make an urgent payment.
Do not transfer money based only on an unexpected message or call.
Independently verify the person or organization before making a payment.

PHISHING LINKS
Scammers may send links that imitate banking, shopping, government, or other legitimate websites.
Do not click suspicious links or enter passwords, card details, OTPs, or other sensitive information on unknown websites.
Check the website address carefully and use the organization's official website instead.

PRIZE AND REWARD SCAMS
Messages claiming that you have won a lottery, prize, cashback, or reward may be designed to make you pay a fee or reveal personal information.
Do not pay money to claim an unexpected prize.
Verify the promotion through the organization's official website.

SENSITIVE INFORMATION
Requests for passwords, PINs, CVV numbers, card numbers, Aadhaar numbers, PAN details, or other sensitive information should be treated cautiously.
Never disclose sensitive credentials to an unknown person or unverified website.

GENERAL SAFETY
Do not act under pressure.
Do not share OTPs or passwords.
Do not click suspicious links.
Do not transfer money to unknown recipients.
Verify unexpected requests using an independent official channel.
